What it is

Every square holds a number. Every wall between two squares holds an operation. Trace a path from the doorway to the exit, and the running total you carry out has to match the target.

Cross a × wall and your total multiplies by the number you land on. Cross a ÷ wall and it divides — but only if the answer comes out whole. If it does not, that wall is shut.

Which means the board changes shape depending on the number you are carrying. A gate closed at 7 opens at 14. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and we prove it before you ever see the board.

No timers forcing you to rush, no lives to run out of, and nothing you have to buy to finish a level. A puzzle a day, or an hour at a time — both work.
