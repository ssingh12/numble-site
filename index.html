What it is

numble is a puzzle game about paths and arithmetic. Every grid is a small maze of numbers, and every puzzle asks the same question: which route through it adds up to the number at the top of the screen?

There are no timers forcing you to rush, no lives to run out of, and nothing you have to buy to finish a level. A puzzle a day, or an hour at a time — both work.
