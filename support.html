Playing the game

How do I play?

Each puzzle is a grid of numbers. Trace a path from the entry to the exit by dragging your finger across the grid. The numbers along your path combine to make a running total, and your goal is to finish on the target shown at the top of the screen.

My path will not go where I want it to

Paths follow the openings between cells, and cannot cross themselves. If a move is refused, there is either a wall in the way or you have already visited that cell. Lift your finger to start again.

What are hints?

Hints reveal part of the solution. You get up to three per puzzle, and each one used reduces your score for that level.

How is my score worked out?

Your score combines a base score for the level with a time bonus, minus a penalty for each hint used. Finishing quickly and without hints earns three stars.

Account and progress

Do I need an account?

No. You can play everything without registering. An account lets your scores appear on the leaderboard and keeps your progress if you change device.

I signed in and my progress disappeared

Progress is stored on the device you played on. If you played without an account and then signed in on a different device, the original device still holds that progress. Email us if scores are missing from a device you have signed in on.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Profile and choose Delete account. This removes your sign-in record, every score we hold for you, and the progress cached on that device. It cannot be undone.

How do I turn off personalised ads?

Through your device settings — on iOS, Settings → Privacy → Tracking; on Android, Settings → Google → Ads. numble is configured to show G-rated advertising only.

Still stuck?

Write to support@numble.app and tell us what happened. We would rather hear about a problem than have you delete the app.
