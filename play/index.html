numbleNimble with Numbers

This is a phone game.

So it is running in a phone. What is in the frame is the real app — the same puzzle generator, the same boards, nothing simplified for the web.

  1. Drag from the green doorway to trace a path. A mouse works as well as a finger — press, drag, release.
  2. Every wall between two squares carries an operation. Crossing it applies that operation to the number you land on.
  3. A gate is shut unless crossing it leaves a whole number of at least zero. So the board changes shape depending on the number you are carrying — a gate closed at 7 opens at 14.
  4. Reach the gold exit carrying exactly the target.

Your progress is saved in this browser. Close the tab and come back — it will be here. Only clearing your browsing data removes it. There are no accounts and nothing to sign up for.

A browser has no vibration motor and no ad support, so the haptics and the ads are simply absent here.
